Each teacher enters grades for their own subject in their class sheet.

How to use
6
1. Open the tab for your class at the bottom (e.g. 'Grade 1A').
7
2. Find your subject's column (the headers are the subject names).
8
3. Fill in the green teacher block under your subject: Full Name, Email, Birth Date, Phone, Address and Subject.
9
4. Type each student's grade in the yellow cells of your column only.
10
5. Leave the cell EMPTY if a grade is not available/absent. Do not type text or 0 for absent.
11
6. Grades must be numbers from 0 up to the max grade shown at the top of your class tab: 25 for Grades 1-3, 20 for Grades 4-9 (decimals allowed).
12
7. Grades below the pass mark (50% of the max grade) turn red automatically. The 'Average' column calculates itself.
13
8. The 'Summary' tab shows what percent of grades are filled in for each class and subject.
14
9. Save the file and send it back (or enter in your own copy of the class sheet only).
15
10. Print report cards from the 'Report Cards' tab: one A4 page per student, filled in automatically from the grades.
